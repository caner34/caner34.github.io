RAG Evaluation Testbed

Compares retrieval strategies and measures where they succeed or fail.

Different retrieval strategies fail in different ways. This testbed compares BM25, deterministic dense retrieval, hybrid fusion, reranking, citations, refusal behavior, and answer-level checks on a small reproducible corpus.

Engineering choice: hybrid retrieval is not always better; the benchmark is designed to make that visible.
